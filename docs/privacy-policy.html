Privacy Policy for Game Vault Last updated: August 27, 2026 Game Vault ("we", "our", "the app") is a rewards app that allows users to earn in-game currency by watching video advertisements. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Information We Collect Account information: email address and password (handled securely via our authentication provider) when you register or log in. Ad-watch activity: which ads you watch, when, and for how long, in order to credit rewards accurately and prevent fraudulent activity. Withdrawal/payout information: the in-game ID (e.g., game UID) and payment details you provide when requesting a payout, solely to process that payout. Advertising identifiers: our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may collect device advertising identifiers and other technical information to serve and measure ads. See Google's own privacy policy below for details on how they handle this data. Basic usage data: general app interaction data used to maintain and improve the app's functionality and security. How We Use Information To create and manage your account. To track ad-watch progress and credit in-game currency rewards. To process withdrawal/payout requests you submit. To detect and prevent fraudulent or automated (bot) activity. To communicate important account or payout notifications to you within the app. Third-Party Services We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices: Supabase — backend database and authentication provider used to store account and app data securely. Google AdMob — advertising network used to serve rewarded video ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on their data practices. Data Retention We retain account and ad-watch data for as long as your account remains active, and as needed to comply with our legal and fraud-prevention obligations. You may request account and data deletion at any time (see Contact section below). Children's Privacy Game Vault is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Your Choices You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us below. You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings, where supported. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
